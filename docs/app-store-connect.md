## Privacy Choices

- Users choose albums, receiver, destination, schedule, and deletion mode.
- Pairing can be forgotten, source state reset, logs cleared, and destination
  files deleted independently.
- Delete After Sync is default-off, system-confirmed, and limited to receiver-
  confirmed assets. Its library-wide effect is disclosed.

## App Privacy

- Provisional answer: Data Not Collected by the developer.
- Photos, videos, identifiers, and transfer metadata are handled on-device and
